      The verdict

      Can you call the next hot sector? No.

      Beyond that one month of momentum — which our system already rides — leadership is effectively unforecastable. Relative-strength acceleration and within-sector breadth, the usual “early” tells, showed no predictive lead. And the one thread that looked promising — a hint of three-month mean-reversion — did not survive a clean test: measured as a real top-vs-bottom forward-return spread, it’s a statistically-insignificant −0.8% (every horizon |t| < 2). The scary early number was a bounded-rank artifact. There is no tradeable sector-timing edge here, in either direction.

      Where it clearly earns its keep is context: a Rotating Bull runs on Technology, Energy and Basic Materials trading the lead; defensive regimes hand it to Staples and Utilities. That map is the shareable, authority-building story.
