## LinkedIn (optional — authority audience)

We spent a decade watching sector leadership rotate, then asked the honest question: can you predict the next hot sector?

The answer is no — and we think saying so is the whole point.

Momentum in sector leadership has a half-life of about a month. Beyond that, every "early tell" we tested (relative-strength acceleration, within-sector breadth, mean-reversion) failed to clear statistical significance as a real forward-return spread.

What *does* hold up is context. Market regime maps cleanly to who leads: Rotating Bulls favor Technology, Energy and Materials; defensive regimes reward Staples and Utilities.

We don't build our process on prediction. We build it on riding the one month of momentum that's real and staying disciplined about the regime we're in.
