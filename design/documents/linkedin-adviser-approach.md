# LinkedIn — Adviser/RIA Channel Sketch (draft Jul 10 2026)

> LinkedIn is NOT a good D2C sub channel (CPCs $8–15+, interruption not intent — it
> would hit the same 0-conversion wall as Google, but pricier). Its ONE real edge is
> **precise job-title targeting** Google can't do → use it for the **adviser/RIA
> partnership motion** (Paul's read: independent RIAs are the channel, position as a
> 10–20% sleeve). This is a slower B2B play, not a quick-sub fix.

## Why LinkedIn for advisers specifically
- Target by **exact job title / firm type** — impossible on Google Search.
- The adviser sale is a *partnership*, not a $129/mo impulse buy — a feed ad that
  starts a relationship (whitepaper, demo, call) fits LinkedIn's format.
- Credibility hurdle (no live record) matters less to an adviser evaluating a
  *methodology + sleeve framework* than to a cold retail buyer handing over a CC.

## Targeting
- **Job titles:** Financial Advisor, Investment Advisor, Wealth Manager, Portfolio
  Manager, Registered Investment Advisor, Financial Planner, CFP
- **Exclude:** wirehouse-only titles won't approve (RJ/ML/MS) — but you can't cleanly
  filter firm; instead target **"RIA" / independent** via firm-name and group targeting
- **Groups/Interests:** RIA, fee-only planning, CFP Board, XY Planning Network,
  quantitative investing
- **Company size:** small independent RIAs (1–50) over wirehouses
- **Geo:** US
- **Seniority:** Owner / Partner / Director (decision-makers at small RIAs)

## Offer / creative (B2B — do NOT sell $129/mo here)
The ad's job is to start a conversation, not close a sub. Lead magnet options:
- **"The Sleeve Framework for Advisers"** one-pager / whitepaper (gated by email)
- **/for-advisers** page as the destination (already built — pivot to sleeve framing)
- **Book a 15-min walkthrough** (Calendly) for the highest-intent

### Angle (matches Paul's feedback)
- Career-risk asymmetry: advisers fear bull-market underperformance more than crash
  participation → position as a **10–20% behavioral sleeve**, not a standalone bet.
- Steal the **F1 analogy** (brakes/cornering/control > raw speed).
- Honesty about multi-year relative underperformance windows = credibility with pros.
- Emphasize: **signals only, adviser keeps discretion & custody** — no RIA competition.

### Sample ad copy (LinkedIn single-image)
- **Headline:** "A behavioral sleeve for the clients who panic-sell"
- **Body:** "Your alpha isn't asset selection — it's keeping clients invested through
  drawdowns. RigaCap is a rules-based momentum sleeve (10–20%) with a 21-year
  walk-forward record and a defensive setting for capitulations. Signals only; you keep
  discretion. See the adviser framework →"
- **CTA:** Learn More → /for-advisers

## Format & budget
- **Format:** Single-image or document ad (whitepaper) — cheaper than video, fits B2B
- **Objective:** Lead gen (LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms — pre-filled, low friction) OR
  website visit to /for-advisers
- **Budget:** LinkedIn min ~$10/day/campaign; expect **$8–15 CPC, $6–12 CPL**. Start
  small ($15–20/day), measure cost-per-adviser-lead, not cost-per-sub.
- **Success metric:** qualified adviser conversations, not subscriptions. Different KPI.

## Sequencing (do this AFTER the D2C funnel is diagnosed)
1. Fix/prove the core funnel first (why zero subs) — don't open a 2nd paid channel
   while the funnel is unproven.
2. Revamp /for-advisers per Paul (re-entry trigger, 60/40 sleeve illustration,
   underperformance honesty, client-ed one-pagers) — the LZ has to be ready.
3. Build the sleeve whitepaper lead magnet.
4. THEN run a small LinkedIn lead-gen test to advisers.
