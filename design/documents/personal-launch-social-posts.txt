FACEBOOK  (personal feed — warm, story-first; the link is clickable here)
========================================================================

I don't post here often — so when I do, it usually means something.

This started as an idea 15 years ago. Honestly? It's been on and off ever
since — something I'd pick up, get pulled away from, and keep coming back to.
This year I finally committed to finishing it. Today it's live: RigaCap
(rigacap.com).

The idea behind it: most people don't lose money in the market because they
picked bad stocks. They lose to their own timing — panic-selling at the
bottom, freezing at the top. The instinct that feels like protecting yourself
is usually the moment the damage gets locked in.

So I built a system that makes the hold-or-sell call on a rule instead of a
gut feeling. It rides momentum when the market rewards it, and steps to cash —
automatically — when things truly break down. One engine, two settings: dial
it toward protect or toward grow, depending on the kind of investor you are.

It's not advice and it's not a magic button — it's signals, and you place the
trades at your own broker. But it's the tool I always wished existed, and it
was tested honestly through the ugly years (2008, 2022), not just the good
ones.

If you — or someone you know — invests and has ever agonized over "should I
sell right now?", take a look: rigacap.com. There's a 7-day free trial, and
I'd genuinely value your honest feedback. 🙏
